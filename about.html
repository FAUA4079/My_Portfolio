About Me

Get to know the person behind the terminal.

[ Focus & Trajectory ]

My journey in cybersecurity is driven by a relentless curiosity to understand and secure the technologies of tomorrow. Currently, my primary focus is dedicated to Web and AI Penetration Testing. I spend my time analyzing the unique attack vectors introduced by large language models (LLMs) and dissecting complex web application frameworks. I actively apply these advanced concepts as a Cyber Security Engineering undergraduate at the University of Frontier Technology, balancing my academic foundation with intensive, practical application.


My dedication to this specialized path is reflected in my recent milestones. To formalize my expertise in emerging threats, I earned the Certified LLM Security Expert (CLLMSE) credential from Red Team Leaders and cleared the CORD certification on Hackviser. These achievements build upon a proven track record of competitive, hands-on exploitation: I currently rank in the Top 2% on TryHackMe with over 80 completed rooms, and hold the "Script Kiddie" rank on Hack The Box, having conquered 17+ active labs and academy modules.


Looking forward, my ultimate goal is to remain at the bleeding edge of offensive security. As artificial intelligence becomes inextricably linked with modern web infrastructure, I am dedicated to discovering, analyzing, and mitigating vulnerabilities at the intersection of these two domains. I aim to uncover the flaws in next-generation platforms before malicious actors do, ensuring that the future of the web is built securely from the ground up.
